ReConDuct: Reusable Kit Market Survey

Good day!

We are the ReConDuct Team conducting a survey for our Applied Economics project under the SDG Business Showcase.

Our proposed product, ReConDuct Kit, is an affordable reusable kit consisting of a water tumbler, lunchbox, spoon and fork, and lunchbag. This product aims to encourage responsible consumption while helping reduce single-use plastic waste in our school.

This survey aims to determine students’ current use of reusable products, their interest in purchasing our proposed kit, and their preferred price range.

Your participation is voluntary. All responses will be used solely for academic purposes. Please answer honestly. Thank you for your time and participation!
